Diagnosing and Fixing a Working-Capital Problem Hiding in Order-to-Cash

RAK Ceramics · Senior Programme Manager — Digital Transformation (PMO) · ~€2M programme · 4 business units · 3 sites

Problem

Leadership saw working capital tied up but assumed it was a demand-planning issue. Diagnosis showed the root cause was fragmented O2C and P2P workflows across four business units and three sites, with manual, error-prone planning cycles masking the real bottleneck.

Solution & Delivery

Designed and led an end-to-end SAP S/4HANA O2C/P2P redesign — introduced MTS supermarket logic, redefined MTO parameters, and optimized MOQs. Partnered with S&OP leadership to integrate SAP IBP with AI-assisted demand forecasting and A/B-tested planning rules, and built PMO governance (stage gates, RAID, steering cadence) with a role-based change programme for 400+ users, using PMP and Agile/Waterfall delivery discipline throughout.

Outcome

€0.7M working capital released, validated against financial actuals. 70% reduction in manual planning overrides. ~20% faster executive reporting via Power BI and an NLP KPI chatbot. 90%+ user adoption within 8 weeks of go-live.

Untangling Engineering Change Management for a Software-Defined Vehicle Programme

Tata Technologies · Client: Tata Motors · Digital Transformation Consultant — PLM & Industry 4.0 · 2,500+ engineers

Problem

Vehicle engineering change management was slow and disputed across teams. Diagnosis traced it to disconnected CAD and PLM environments with no shared source of truth — limiting agility on Tata Motors' Software Defined Vehicle programme.

Solution & Delivery

Designed simulation-integrated PLM workflows and Digital Twin validation environments (Teamcenter + NX/Simcenter + AR/VR), and led the Teamcenter BOM governance, DMU validation and CAD integration (PTC Creo, Siemens NX) rollout across multi-disciplinary teams. Partnered with engineering leadership on the investment case, and led a Generative AI pilot for engineering documentation with Python analytics pipelines to surface bottlenecks and target the change management ahead of time.

Outcome

35% engineering efficiency gain across 2,500+ engineers, presented and validated at board level. 18% PLM data accuracy improvement and 12% reduction in engineering change cycle time. 10% reduction in design cycle time and 25% improvement in platform adoption through role-specific OCM.

Nov 2020 – Sep 2022

Building an FDA/ISO-Regulated Engineering Function — and Team — from Zero

Conformis / Restor3D · Engineering CAD Lead & Project Coordinator · $14M programme insourced · Greenfield build

Problem

No local CAD/MES delivery capability existed for patient-specific orthopaedic implants — the work, and the risk, sat with a third-party vendor (L&T Technologies) instead of in-house, with no team to insource it to.

Solution & Delivery

Designed the delivery model and project governance for patient-specific implant design (SolidWorks, reverse engineering from CT scan data), built CAD-to-MES handover and automated BOM generation — and led the build of the team itself, scaling engineering from zero through structured on-the-job training and competency programmes I designed and ran.

Outcome

$14M+ of annual volume fully insourced. Team scaled to 80+ engineers in 24 months, cutting ramp time by 62%. 650+ implants delivered annually in an FDA 21 CFR Part 820 / ISO 13485 environment with zero critical audit findings — earning the company's first-ever Outstanding Performer Award.

Smart Manufacturing · India
78%
OEE, up from 63.5%
Aug 2018 – Oct 2020

Diagnosing an OEE Gap on a 100,000-Unit-a-Year Production Line

Linkwell Telesystems · Lean & DFX Engineer (MCAD Design Engineer)

Problem

An energy-meter line running 100,000 units/year was underperforming on Overall Equipment Effectiveness, limiting throughput and margin — with the root cause spread across process, design and cross-team handoffs.

Solution & Delivery

Led Value Stream Mapping and Lean cell redesign of the production line using Lean Six Sigma method. Rolled out SAP ERP and Teamcenter PLM across 10+ cross-functional teams, and introduced DFMEA and FEA controls with OEE dashboards to close design-stage risk before production release.

Outcome

OEE lifted from 63.5% to 78%, delivering Rs. 5M in annual savings — recognized with the Best Team Award for transformation impact.
